Traceroute Analyzer

Paste tracert, traceroute or mtr output — identify who owns every hop (ASN, IXP, location, reverse DNS) and tell real latency or loss from ICMP rate-limiting.

Split view

Parsing and private / CGNAT checks run locally. With online lookups on, each public hop is queried straight from your browser: RIPEstat (BGP origin & prefix), PeeringDB (IXP peering LANs), ipwho.is / ipapi.co (location) and Cloudflare DoH (reverse DNS).
